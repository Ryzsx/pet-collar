Features

Everything they need. Everything you need to know.

One smart collar designed to help you understand, protect, and care for your pet every day.

◎

Adaptive GPS & Safe Zones

Track your pet’s location using activity-based GPS updates and set safe zones for location monitoring and geofence alerts.

✦

Activity & Wellness Monitoring

Classifies resting, walking, and running activities and identifies significant deviations from your pet’s established activity pattern.

Smart Notifications

Receive alerts for safe zone exits, low battery, collar connection loss, restored connection, and important device status changes.

✓

Offline Data Logging

Stores activity and device records locally when connectivity is unavailable and synchronizes them when the connection is restored.

Dashboard

Your pet's world, at a glance.

Everything your smart collar collects is organized in one simple dashboard. Check your pet's activity, alerts, and location without digging through complicated menus.
